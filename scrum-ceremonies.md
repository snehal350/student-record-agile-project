## Daily Scrum
Each Developer briefly discusses:
- What progress did I make toward the Sprint Goal?
- What will I work on next?
- Is anything blocking progress?

Record blockers and owners; use a separate discussion for detailed problem-solving.

- What went well?
- What did not go well?
- What did we learn?
- What one or two improvements will we try next sprint?
- Who will help make each improvement happen?
