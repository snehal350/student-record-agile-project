# Scrum Team Structure and Responsibilities

## Proposed team structure
Replace role assignments with actual team members' names before submission.

| Scrum accountability / role in assignment | Suggested assignment | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Product Owner | Team member 1 | Clarifies requirements, orders the Product Backlog, represents stakeholder needs, and reviews outcomes. |
| Scrum Master | Team member 2 | Helps the team understand Scrum, facilitates events, and helps remove impediments. |
| Developers | Remaining team members | Create a usable increment, collaborate on design and implementation, test work, and maintain documentation. |

## Collaboration agreements
- Discuss blockers early.
- Keep backlog items small and understandable.
- Agree on acceptance criteria before starting work.
- Review completed work against acceptance criteria.
- Do not mark an item complete until the team agrees it meets its Definition of Done.

## Definition of Done (proposed)
A backlog item is Done when:
- The acceptance criteria are met.
- Relevant tests or checks have been performed.
- Documentation is updated where needed.
- No known critical issue prevents the feature from being used.
- The result has been reviewed by the team.

Note: Scrum uses the accountabilities Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers. The table above uses the assignment's familiar team-role wording while retaining that distinction.
