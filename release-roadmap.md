# Project Timeline and Release Roadmap

This is a proposed sequence, not a confirmed calendar. Replace the relative phases with dates that match your course schedule.

| Phase | Focus | Main outputs | Exit check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery and planning | Confirm vision, stakeholders, epics, stories, and risks | Product vision and prioritized backlog | Stakeholders understand the MVP |
| Sprint 1 | Project setup and authentication/access design | Foundation and access workflow | Login and permissions criteria reviewed |
| Sprint 2 | Student management | Add, search, update, deactivate student | Student workflows meet criteria |
| Sprint 3 | Courses and attendance | Course assignment and attendance | Records can be checked and reported |
| Sprint 4 | Marks and reporting | Marks, grades, result sheets, attendance report | Calculations follow approved rules |
| Later release | Notifications and analytics | Alerts and expanded reports | Stakeholder feedback supports release |

## Release strategy
- **MVP:** authentication, student records, course management, attendance, marks/results, and basic reports.
- **Enhancement release:** notifications and expanded reports.
- **Future consideration:** advanced analytics and integrations.

## Roadmap review
Review priorities after each Sprint Review. Do not promise release dates until the team has confirmed scope, capacity, and dependencies.
